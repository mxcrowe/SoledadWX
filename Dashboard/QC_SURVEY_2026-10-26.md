# Archive QC survey (read-only)

> **Read this first (added after the build).** This is the survey that shaped the QC, not its
> final output. Three things changed once it was examined: spikes are tested *within* one source
> (most "spikes" below are two sources interleaving, not faults); "held" applies only where
> readings are <= 5 min apart (the 20-min Cumulus era stands on single readings); and the
> hygrometer bad periods became Jul 2010-Nov 2016, Aug 2017-Aug 2018 and the 2014 WU proxy.
> Live implementation: `db::refresh_dirty_rollups`.

Generated 2026-10-26 12:04 from the canonical stream (same source priority the pages use). Nothing was changed. `*` marks proxy sources. "Held" = the extreme had to last at least two consecutive readings. Isolation = gap between #1 and #2 ÷ median gap among ranks 2–20 (look above ~3).

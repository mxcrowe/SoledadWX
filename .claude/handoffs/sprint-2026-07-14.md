**Solar caveat (important):** the current WS-1002 solar sensor is flat, south-
tilted, and cup-shadowed by the anemometer — confirmed on the roof. It reads
~50-60% of true GHI, jagged, best at low sun. So all solar (energy, %, records)
is **relative-only, not calibrated**. `SOLAR_CLEAR_CAPTURE = 0.62` in astro.ts
is the single knob; bump toward ~0.9 when the station is upgraded and it becomes
true insolation. Do NOT build an all-time "sunniest day" record (meaningless in
San Diego + biased sensor). Solar not comparable across sources (WU ≈ +13% vs
HP2000). Zephyr (src1) solar/UV are phantom zeros — exclude source 1.

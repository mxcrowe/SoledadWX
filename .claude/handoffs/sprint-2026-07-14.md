## Where things stand
Data engine unchanged from the 07-08 handoff (~17.1M obs, self-healing multi-
source, forensic gap honesty — all still true). This sprint was all UI/features
on top of it.

**Built this sprint (Console + new page):**
- Console: cloudiness estimate, humidity trend, wind chill/heat index (N/A when
  out of NWS range), vector-averaged wind-dir gauge, honest "Peak gust / Max
  sustained" wind block, wind-rose color escalation (15-20 yellow, 20+ red),
  daily **solar energy** (kWh/m²) + **"% of a clear day"**.
- New **Today/Yesterday** page (nav ⇄): Beaumaris-style grouped compare
  (Temperature/Dew/Humidity/Wind/Pressure/Rain/Solar-UV), each row = Today
  (value @time) · Diff · Yesterday (value @time). Gridded (row bands + column
  rules), one large header, bright section labels. Backend: **db::day_compare**.
- Fixes: negative day-length bug (astro.ts), sticky sidebar footer, gauge
  center-label removal + SteelElement rebuild-on-option-change.

**Solar caveat (important):** the current WS-1002 solar sensor is flat, south-
tilted, and cup-shadowed by the anemometer — confirmed on the roof. It reads
~50-60% of true GHI, jagged, best at low sun. So all solar (energy, %, records)
is **relative-only, not calibrated**. `SOLAR_CLEAR_CAPTURE = 0.62` in astro.ts
is the single knob; bump toward ~0.9 when the station is upgraded and it becomes
true insolation. Do NOT build an all-time "sunniest day" record (meaningless in
San Diego + biased sensor). Solar not comparable across sources (WU ≈ +13% vs
HP2000). Zephyr (src1) solar/UV are phantom zeros — exclude source 1.
